XCMate (ursprünglich XtreamMate 0.1.0) — independent Kodi fork, GS Media

Upstream: mr-evil1 / TiViMate Kodi 2026.06.12.1, https://github.com/mr-evil1/kodi-media-repo
Original addon declares GPL-2.0-only. Full GPL v2 license accompanies this fork.
Changes: renamed addon ID, first-run installation wizard, content toggles, scan counters,
Xtream category multiselect, separate userdata and redaction of sensitive log data.

This is an early test build for Kodi 21+. Device-specific remote overlays, 2-stage
Back/Left remote navigation, and full poster file prefetch are NOT yet implemented.
The VOD and series response data and image URLs are cached by the upstream client;
posters are fetched by Kodi as needed and are not predownloaded en masse.
There is no video source or login embedded in this package. Use authorized services.

2026-10-14: Visible product branding changed to XCMate at release 0.1.9. Internal Kodi addon ID `plugin.video.xtreammate` unchanged to preserve upgrades and user profiles. Upstream attribution and GPL remain unchanged.

2026-10-18: XCMate 0.1.22 test candidate: explicit Kodi 21 WindowDialog control construction and onControl callbacks, single native navigation/selection, bundled focus textures, playback ownership cleanup, writable-cache fallback, per-programme XMLTV validation, bounded streaming HTTP/GZIP and asynchronous manual EPG refresh. Existing accounts, mappings and add-on identity preserved. Android-TV acceptance pending.
